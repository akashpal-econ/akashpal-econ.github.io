## Research

My research lies at the intersection of **health economics, household economics, and applied microeconomics**. I am particularly interested in understanding how health risks, healthcare costs, and socioeconomic conditions shape individual and household decisions.

### Health Economics

My research in health economics focuses on healthcare utilisation, health-seeking behaviour, healthcare expenditure, health insurance, and financial protection.

I am interested in understanding how households respond to health shocks and how healthcare costs affect economic well-being, particularly in developing-country settings.

### Occupational Health

A major strand of my current research examines **occupational health in India**, with a particular focus on **silicosis**.

My work investigates how silicosis is represented in Indian policy discourse and examines the broader socioeconomic and policy dimensions of occupational disease.

### Household Economics

I am interested in household-level decision-making related to health, healthcare utilisation, expenditure, and financial risk.

This research explores how socioeconomic characteristics and health conditions influence household behaviour and welfare.

### Applied Microeconomics

My empirical research uses quantitative methods to investigate policy-relevant questions in health and household economics. I am particularly interested in applied microeconometric approaches, survey data, and text-based methods.

### Methods

My methodological interests include:

* Applied microeconometrics
* Causal inference
* Survey-data analysis
* Text-data analysis
* Structural topic modelling
* Bibliometric analysis
* Quantitative policy analysis
