An online session introducing the concepts and methods of **bibliometric analysis**, with a focus on the practical use of bibliographic databases and analytical tools. The session covered **search strategy and query formulation in Scopus**, including the use of Boolean operators, keywords, and search fields for identifying relevant literature.

The practical component introduced bibliographic data retrieval from **Scopus, Web of Science, and PubMed**, along with data cleaning and merging records across databases. Hands-on exercises were conducted using **R and the Bibliometrix package** to examine publication trends, citation patterns, collaboration networks, keyword co-occurrence, and thematic structures.

The session also included an introduction to **VOSviewer** for the visualisation and interpretation of bibliometric networks, including co-authorship, keyword co-occurrence, and citation relationships.
